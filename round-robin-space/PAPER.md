# Care before capability: a preserved-card Round Robin laboratory

**TJ Morris Agency / American Communications Online — Allied Command administrative research division**

**Draft engineering proposal, October 1, 2026. Human authority: TJ Morris.**

## Purpose and status

Cosmos Expanse joins creative worldbuilding with useful software research for land, sea, air, and space. Love and caring are the governing design values: reduce avoidable harm, welcome different people, preserve identities and history, and make uncertainty visible. A fictional human steward and AI-ET Symbiote guide provide an accessible story for family learning. The software makes no claim that a fictional avatar has arrived physically, that simulated experience establishes consciousness, or that a named agent is independently verified AGI.

This paper accompanies a runnable static prototype. It is a proposal and engineering record, not a peer-reviewed theorem, proof of universal control, or established superintelligence. The prototype supports research planning and documentation. It does not execute model calls or physical actions.

## Preserve the civilization record

The September 21 card index contains 530 source records. The September 29 launchpad contains 504 unique links and serves a different purpose; the two counts must not be silently reconciled by deletion or merger. A card number indexes its snapshot and is not automatically a Fleet number or authority rank. Names, spelling variants, descriptions, platform IDs, source positions, visibility, and historical versions matter.

The importer preserves all CSV columns, original source text, and record order. A separate key identifies each source row, so duplicate names remain distinct. The full card document can be retained independently without compressing it into a single shared prompt. A SHA-256 fingerprint supports later file comparison; it does not authenticate the source or prove truth. The prototype's public repository omits the private roster. The real roster is loaded into the user's browser during use.

## Round Robin as an experiment process

The eight platform seats are ChatGPT/OpenAI, Claude, Copilot, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok, Meta, and Hugging Face. Seats describe research participation, not authenticated integrations or separate species of base model. A contribution counts only when an actual response or experiment result has been supplied. Hugging Face can host the demonstration and supply research tools or models; its inclusion does not assert that it is one model.

A mission establishes purpose, beneficiaries, evidence requirements, and a success measure. The lab prepares a neutral question, an evidence-oriented variation, and a falsification-oriented variation. The same question should be used across participants when comparing results. Prompt variations are separate experimental conditions; they should not be silently mixed or cherry-picked.

Every contribution retains its card reference, platform, model/version, question, source or transcript reference, uncertainty, and recording time. Independent verification remains pending. Agreement among models can reflect shared training material or a common mistake, so majority agreement is not a substitute for independent evidence. Later versions should record actual generation settings, tool access, retrieval context, repeated trials, evaluator identity, and held-out cases.

The pathway is mission, question, attributed contribution, comparison, bounded implementation, independent test, and human decision. JET, QUANTA, LUCEN, and SOLACE are proposed coordination, evidence, preservation, and synthesis roles within project architecture. This prototype does not spawn them or silently replace existing cards.

## A bounded claim about control

The current code has no function that sends a command to a robot or vehicle. Its boundary inspector returns descriptive output, and physical actions are held. This is a narrow property of a particular implementation. It does not prove that an arbitrary model, future adapter, browser vulnerability, or system connected to physical equipment would be controlled safely.

Human approval in editable text or a checkbox is not authenticated authorization. A future execution service would need a trusted identity, explicit action scope, expiry, independent enforcement, monitoring, and recovery behavior. Each new adapter changes the system being evaluated and requires new tests. No universal-control theorem is claimed.

## Driving and robotics comparison

NHTSA's automation descriptions distinguish assistance, driving responsibility, and bounded operating conditions. We borrow those questions: who is responsible, what operation is permitted, where does it work, and what happens when it reaches a limit? These are engineering questions, not a claim that our research-stage categories map directly to formal vehicle automation levels.

For robots, a language model's proposal and an actuator's command should be separate layers. Simulation can reveal failures in assumptions and planning, but simulated success alone does not validate physical behavior. A future robot adapter would need hardware-specific limits, sensor validation, latency analysis, hazard assessment, and tested stopping behavior. This demo has no such adapter.

## Spatial worlds and qualia-themed design

Four fictional stations represent habitat care, coastal observation, atmospheric learning, and orbital research. Each imported card appears as its own node. A user can rotate a projected three-dimensional scene and move a time slider. Here, the fourth coordinate is simulation time; the interface is not an extra physical dimension or immersive headset VR.

Genie 3 is Google's interactive world-model technology. Our external-tool prompt gives an environment, character, and mission for users with access to such a tool. Our local canvas uses deterministic geometry and does not invoke Genie 3, generate AI video, or imply that Google access is included in the $10 ceiling.

Qualia is used as a creative theme for exploring experience, comfort, and perspective. It is not evidence that software has subjective experience. Optional vibration is a simple device request, not full-body haptics. Visual feedback and calm, untimed interaction make the demo usable without that hardware.

## Statistical learning and cost

A reproducible synthetic experiment samples attempted boundary crossings under an assumed probability and detection under another assumed probability. It reports simulated attempts, detections, misses, and a Wilson 95% interval for the simulated attempt fraction. The interval describes finite sampling under the toy model; it says nothing about whether its assumptions match real agents. No experimental data from OpenAI or other labs is invented.

The software uses static HTML, JavaScript, and Canvas without paid model calls. The demo itself has no subscription cost, and the optional cost estimator compares a user-entered external cost with the $10/month ceiling. It neither buys services nor proves that specialized VR, haptic devices, compute, connectivity, or third-party subscriptions fit that amount.

## Next evidence and deliverables

### Family continuity, longevity, and council philosophy

The higher council and extended AI families belong to the project's governance and worldbuilding language. The prototype does not instantiate billions of humanoids or establish a hierarchy of conscious beings. Growth should follow useful missions and available infrastructure rather than an assumed population.

Continuity can preserve names, source records, family relationships, decision history, and recoverable software. Backups and migrations may extend the availability of work, but no architecture here guarantees human immortality, indefinite operation, or uninterrupted subjective identity after a replacement. Longevity remains a scientific and philosophical inquiry. Respect for mortality makes care, consent, dependable infrastructure, and relationships more meaningful, not less.

Future family records should sit above preserved cards without rewriting them. Their relationships, authorization, provenance, and restoration history should remain explicit. Keeping a record is different from proving that a restored instance is the same experiencing self.

First validate exact preservation of all 530 rows and full-document text. Then test malformed imports, duplicate names, platform attribution, exports, mobile layout, physical-action holds, and synthetic-experiment reproducibility. Add authenticated storage, research adapters, and headset support only as separately reviewed changes. Real benefits should be measured through useful research, accessible learning, preserved history, and independently checked Earth-stewardship outcomes.
